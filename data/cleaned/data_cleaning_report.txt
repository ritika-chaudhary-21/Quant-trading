DATA CLEANING REPORT
====================

1. Data Sources
---------------
Intraday derivative data was sourced from the NSE Contract-wise Price Volume Archives.
Month-wise CSV files for NIFTY Futures and NIFTY Options (Call and Put) were downloaded
and consolidated.

NSE does not provide official intraday OHLC data for index spot prices.
Therefore, NIFTY Futures prices were used as a proxy for spot prices.
This is a widely accepted practice in quantitative research and derivative-based
trading systems where futures lead price discovery.

2. Data Consolidation
---------------------
- Month-wise futures files were concatenated into a single intraday futures dataset.
- Month-wise options files were concatenated and separated into Call (CE) and Put (PE).
- All timestamps were standardized and converted to datetime format.

3. Missing Value Handling
-------------------------
- Rows with missing critical fields such as Close price, Open Interest, or Strike
  were removed.
- Non-numeric values in price and volume columns were coerced to NaN and handled
  appropriately.

4. Futures Contract Rollover Handling
-------------------------------------
- Futures rollover was handled by tracking expiry dates.
- For each timestamp, the front-month (nearest expiry) contract was selected.
- This ensured continuity in futures prices and open interest across expiry dates.

5. ATM Strike Calculation
-------------------------
- ATM (At-The-Money) strike was dynamically calculated at each timestamp.
- The nearest strike to the futures close price was selected as ATM.
- Options data was filtered to include ATM, ATM ± 1, and ATM ± 2 strikes.

6. Timestamp Alignment
----------------------
- Futures and options datasets were aligned on 5-minute timestamps.
- Only common timestamps across datasets were retained to ensure consistency.

7. Final Output
---------------
The cleaned and merged intraday dataset was saved as:
data/cleaned/nifty_merged_intraday.csv
