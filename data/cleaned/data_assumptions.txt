DATA ASSUMPTIONS
================

1. Spot Price Proxy
-------------------
NSE does not publish official intraday OHLC data for index spot prices.
To overcome this limitation, NIFTY Futures prices were used as a proxy for
spot prices.

This approach is widely used in:
- Intraday derivatives research
- Options pricing models
- Market microstructure studies

2. Risk-Free Rate
-----------------
A constant annualized risk-free rate of 6.5% was assumed for options Greeks
calculations, in line with prevailing Indian government bond yields.

3. Liquidity Assumption
-----------------------
It is assumed that ATM and near-ATM option strikes (ATM ± 2) are sufficiently
liquid for intraday analysis.

4. Transaction Costs
--------------------
Transaction costs and slippage were not included unless explicitly stated
in the strategy backtesting section.
